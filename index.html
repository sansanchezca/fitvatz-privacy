FitVatz Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

FitVatz is a fitness and nutrition app for iPhone, published by Santiago Sanchez as an individual developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

We don't sell your information

FitVatz doesn't sell, rent or trade your personal or health information.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile, workouts, meals, progress and settings are stored only on your device, encrypted with a key kept in the iOS Keychain. FitVatz has no servers that receive this data.

Your account

If you sign in with Apple or Google, or continue as a guest, FitVatz creates an account with Google Firebase Authentication. It keeps an account ID and, if you share them, your name and email address, only to run your account. Your workouts, meals and health data are not uploaded. You can delete your account at any time in Profile; deleting it also revokes Sign in with Apple access.

Apple Health

FitVatz reads only the Health data you allow (for example steps, sleep and heart rate) to tailor your workouts, and writes your workouts and meals to Health if you allow it. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with third parties. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

Camera and meal photos

The camera is used to recognize meals and scan food barcodes. Meal photos are analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device tools. They are not saved by FitVatz and are not uploaded.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, FitVatz uses Face ID through iOS. FitVatz never receives or stores your biometric data.

Artificial intelligence

AI features use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device.

Internet lookups

  • If a food isn't in FitVatz's built-in list, the food name you search is sent to USDA FoodData Central.
  • If you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product.

These lookups do not include your name, your health data or any account information.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. FitVatz requests non-personalized ads and does not ask to track you across other apps and websites. Google may collect certain device and usage information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policies. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, FitVatz may ask for your consent before showing ads. FitVatz never shares health or fitness data with Google or advertisers. FitVatz Pro, when available, removes ads.

Your control

You can erase everything from the Profile screen at any time. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device.

Children

FitVatz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
